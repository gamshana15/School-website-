Student Admissions & Official Certificates

For student admission inquiries, School Leaving Certificates, Character Certificates, and official verification letters, please visit the administrative office during regular school hours with relevant identification and past student records.

School Location Map

Palali Road, Kondavil East, Jaffna, Sri Lanka

How to Reach the School:

The Vidyalayam is conveniently situated along the main Palali Road. Regular public buses travelling from Jaffna Bus Stand towards Palali, Kankesanthurai, and Point Pedro pass directly through Kondavil with a designated stop near the school entrance.
