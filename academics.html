Education Levels

Institutional Grade Structure

A cohesive educational pathway guiding students from initial primary literacy through university qualification.

Grades 1 – 5

Primary School

Foundational literacy, numeracy, Tamil, English, activity-based discovery, moral ethics, and Grade 5 Scholarship mentoring.

Activity-based learning & character building
Grades 6 – 9

Junior Secondary

Tamil, Mathematics, Science, History, Geography, English, Religion, Aesthetic subjects, and Information & Communication Technology.

Science practicals and IT fundamentals
Grades 10 – 11

Senior Secondary (O/L)

Targeted preparation for G.C.E. Ordinary Level examinations, revision seminars, past-paper workshops, and career counseling.

Preparation for Advanced Level admission
Grades 12 – 13

Collegiate (G.C.E. A/L)

Advanced academic streams geared for university admissions in Commerce and Arts.

Easthatic practicals & ICT facilities
Advanced Level

Collegiate Subject Streams

Specialised academic tracks catering to students' career aspirations and university entry.

📊

Commerce Stream

Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, and Information Technology. Prepares students for careers in banking, corporate management, finance, and entrepreneurship.

📜

Arts & Humanities

Tamil, Hindu Culture, Political Science, Geography, History, and Media Studies. Developing critical communication and cultural scholarship.

Educational Approach

Activity-Based Learning & Digital Tools

Reflecting our institutional mission, we emphasize hands-on learning that bridges theoretical knowledge with real-world application:

  • Dedicated Computer Laboratory providing digital literacy and programming fundamentals.
  • Practical Science Laboratory equipped for experiment-driven understanding.
  • School Library fostering reading comprehension and research habits.
  • Structured term examinations, past-paper analysis, and continuous performance review.
  • Parent-teacher consultations to track individual student progress.

National Curriculum & Standardized Portals

All instructional programs follow the approved syllabus of the Ministry of Education Sri Lanka and the National Institute of Education (NIE).
