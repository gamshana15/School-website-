Co-Curricular Life

Clubs & Student Societies

Cultivating communication skills, scientific curiosity, artistic expression, and leadership.

✍️

Tamil Literary Association

Speech, essay writing, poetry recitation, debating forums, and drama workshops aimed at celebrating Tamil linguistic heritage and rhetoric.

🔬

Science Society

Science exhibitions, innovation displays, nature study clubs, and regional science Olympiad preparation.

💻

Information Technology Society

Digital literacy, coding fundamentals, graphic design, and responsible internet safety awareness for students.

🌱

Environmental Society

Tree planting drives, campus cleanliness campaigns, waste management, and environmental awareness initiatives.

🤝

Activity Committee

The official committee coordinating co-curricular competitions, school assemblies, and student assemblies.

Athletics & Games

Sports & Physical Education

Instilling endurance, team spirit, fair play, and physical fitness among students.

🏃‍♂️

Annual Inter-House Sports Meet

A centerpiece of the school calendar, featuring track and field events, march-past displays, drill exhibitions, and awards.

⚾

Elle

Competitive teams representing the school in zonal and divisional tournaments with consistent distinction.

🏐

Volleyball

Outdoor sessions on the school sports field, building team camaraderie and discipline.

Heritage & Tradition

Cultural & Spiritual Celebrations

Upholding traditional Tamil culture, spiritual harmony, and moral values.

🪔

Saraswathi Pooja & Navarathri

Nine days of devotional worship honoring the Goddess of Learning, culminating in Vijayadasami musical and cultural concerts.

🌾

Thai Pongal Harvest Festival

Traditional harvest thanksgiving observed by students and teachers in authentic attire with communal boiling of sweet rice.

🪷

Guru Poosai

Held every April, our Guru Poosai honours Yogar Swami and the Nayanmarkal. Students and staff gather for prayers, Thirumurai hymns and devotional songs, offering gratitude to the saints who light our spiritual path.
