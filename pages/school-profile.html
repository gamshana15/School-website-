Type 1C Provincial School (வகை 1C மாகாணப் பாடசாலை)

Grade 1 through Grade 13 (தரம் 1 முதல் தரம் 13 வரை)

Tamil Medium with English as a Second Language (தமிழ் மொழிமூலம்)

Co-educational / Mixed (கலவன் பாடசாலை)

Nallur Division (நல்லூர் கோட்டம்)
Province:
Northern Province, Sri Lanka (வட மாகாணம்)

Campus Infrastructure & Learning Facilities

The school environment is equipped with essential academic, recreational, and spiritual infrastructure supporting holistic learner development:

💻

ICT Computer Lab

Internet-connected workstations supporting GIT and A/L ICT curricula

🔬

Science Laboratory

Practical science laboratory facilities for junior and secondary experiments

📚

School Library

Comprehensive collection of reference volumes, periodicals, and past papers

🏟️

Athletic Grounds

Sports field accommodating track and field events, football, and cricket

🎭

Main Assembly Hall

Venue for annual prize-givings, aesthetic performances, and seminars

🪔

Prayer Shrine

Morning assemblies, religious observances, and cultural events
